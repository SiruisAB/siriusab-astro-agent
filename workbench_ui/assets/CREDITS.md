# Lyra application image

Original photograph: **LyraCC.jpg**, photographed by **Till Credner** on 16 May 2004.

- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:LyraCC.jpg
- Photographer: http://www.allthesky.com/constellations/visualconstellations.html
- License: **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported**
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).

`lyra-original.jpg` is the original photograph. `lyra-*.png` and `lyra.ico` are
adaptations: cropped to the constellation, brightened, and overlaid with enhanced
constellation lines and star highlights for legibility at icon sizes. These image
adaptations are also licensed under **CC BY-SA 3.0**. No endorsement by the
photographer is implied. The image license applies to these images only.

Rebuild with `.conda/Scripts/python.exe scripts/build_lyra_icons.py` (Pillow).
